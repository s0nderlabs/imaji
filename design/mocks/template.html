Set up imaji for a repo

Three steps, about ten minutes

Your Mind takes the job once, the workflow goes in your repo once, and every release you tag after that comes back as a kit like the one above.

Hand your Mind the job

About two minutes

Open a chat with your own Mind and paste this in. It is the whole job: what a kit is, the JSON to answer with, how to call imaji, and when to skip a release and say why. Your Mind confirms in one sentence.

You will need
  • A Mind on Minds by Animoca Brands
  • Five minutes of its attention
{{JOB}}

Drop the workflow in your repo

About five minutes

Save this as .github/workflows/imaji.yml. It has two jobs: onboarding, which lets your Mind read the repo once and propose your brand, and release, which runs every time you tag one.

Three repository secrets
  • MINDS_API_KEYyour Builder API key, from build.hellominds.ai
  • MIND_IDyour Mind’s id, lowercase
  • IMAJI_KIT_TOKENthe token from step three
{{WORKFLOW}}

Then run onboarding once by hand: Actions, imaji, Run workflow, job: onboard. After that every release runs it on its own.

Mint a kit token

About a minute

The only credential imaji issues. It lets your Mind post kits and belongs in your repository secrets. Your kit pages live at a read id derived from it, so a kit link is safe to share.
