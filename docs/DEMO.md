# Demo video script (target 1:45, hard cap 2:00)

Screen capture only. No face, no voice-over: on-screen text and the product carry it. imaji announcing imaji, or more exactly a Mind announcing imaji: the repository in the demo is this one, the Mind is `imaji.labs`, the release is v0.3.0, which is a launch.

Every beat names what is on screen and the caption that sits over it.

## Beat 1, the sentence (0:00 to 0:10)

Screen: the front door, `/`, at rest, the v0.2.0 film playing in the fold.
Caption: "A developer who ships is a creator whose content is code."
Then: "Your own Mind turns each release into the posts, the card, the film and the launch video you never had time to make. imaji renders them."

## Beat 2, hand your Mind the job (0:10 to 0:28)

Screen: the Minds chat with `imaji.labs`. The job pasted, cut to the Mind's one-sentence confirmation (recorded earlier; never paste live).
Caption: "You hire your own Mind. imaji is the job description and the hands."
Screen: the front door's setup, the three steps, then the line "Using an agent? Copy the prompt." and a coding agent running the four commands from `/agents.md`.
Caption: "Three steps by hand, or one prompt for your agent."

## Beat 3, tag a release (0:28 to 0:40)

Screen: terminal, `gh release create v0.3.0 --notes-file docs/releases/v0.3.0.md`, Enter. The Actions run starts. A glance at `imaji.json` in the repo (six outputs, look editorial, launch on major).
Caption: "Tag a release. That is the whole prompt."

## Beat 4, the wait, cut (0:40 to 0:50)

Screen: the Action log at "waiting for imaji.labs". Title card: "3 minutes later". Then the log line with the kit URL.
Caption: "Nobody prompted it. Your Mind reads the notes, remembers v0.2.0, writes the kit and the storyboard, and calls the renderer."

## Beat 5, the kit lands (0:50 to 1:25)

Screen: the kit page for v0.3.0. Scroll slowly: the launch video playing (muted, let it run about 12 s: wordmark, the line, the live capture of the homepage panning), then the card, the film and the vertical cut side by side, the tweet, the thread, the LinkedIn post, "What the Mind kept", "Left out".
Caption over the launch video: "A launch video from your repo and your homepage. Rendered from HTML, on brand every time."
Caption at the vertical: "Landscape for X and LinkedIn, vertical for Shorts and Reels."
Caption at the tweet: "In your voice, not a template's."

## Beat 6, the memory moment (1:25 to 1:45)

Screen: the Minds chat. "What do you remember about my releases?" (cut to the answer, recorded earlier). The answer lists v0.1.0, v0.2.0 and v0.3.0 and what changed, and the brand correction it kept.
Caption: "Your Mind remembers every release and every correction. The next kit builds on this one."
Close on the front door with the URL.
Caption: "imaji. A job your Mind can take. imaji.s0nderlabs.xyz"

## What to record before editing

- The Mind's confirmation (beat 2), the kit URL log line (beat 4), the memory answer (beat 6): recorded ahead of time from real runs, then cut in, because each takes 30 s to 3 min live.
- The v0.3.0 kit, with launch and vertical done, must exist on the public host before recording beat 5.
- Record at 1920x1080, browser at 100 percent zoom, light theme for the pages, videos play inline muted.

## Rules

- Under two minutes, or the entry is void.
- No claims beyond what the recording shows.
- Say "Mind" not "agent" on screen for the Minds side; "your coding agent" for the setup helper; say "your Mind" not "our Mind".
