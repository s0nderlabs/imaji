## imaji v0.2.0

The film. Every release now gets a ten-second launch video next to the card.

### What changed

- **Film rendering**: a 1920x1080, 10-second film in four beats (wordmark, thesis, what changed, sign-off), rendered frame by frame from the same HTML composition system as the card. Starts in the background right after the card, lands on the kit page when done.
- **Kit page**: shows the film with a rendering state that resolves on its own, plus copy buttons for the tweet, the thread and the LinkedIn post.
- **Kit index**: `/k/<token>` lists every kit for your token, newest first.
- **Onboarding**: the Mind now proposes the brand from your repo and renders a sample card before the first release.

### Fixes

- Long headlines step down in size instead of overflowing the card.
- Literal `\n\n` in the Mind's copy are rendered as paragraphs.
