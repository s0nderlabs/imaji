## imaji v0.1.0

First release. imaji turns a GitHub release into a launch kit, written by your own Mind and rendered by imaji.

### What's in it

- **The job description** (`job/JOB.md`): paste it once into a chat with your Mind. It explains the kit, the rules, and how to call the renderer.
- **The workflow** (`templates/imaji.yml`): two jobs. `onboard` gathers your README, package description, logo and colour tokens so the Mind proposes your brand and renders a sample card. `release` runs on every published release, sends the notes to your Mind, waits for the kit, and prints the private kit URL.
- **The renderer**: `POST /api/render` takes the kit JSON and produces a 1200x630 social card from HTML. Deterministic, on brand, no image model.
- **Private kit pages**: every kit lands at `/k/<token>/<tag>`. Nothing is posted anywhere.

### Notes

- Each user runs their own Mind. imaji never shares one.
- Kit tokens are minted on the front door and scope your kit pages.
